# Numberin ✨

An esoteric analysis workbench, astronomical calendar, and canonical cross-examination engine built in Streamlit. **Numberin** bridges ancient sacred geometry, linguistic gematria traditions, and real-time celestial cycles into practical, down-to-earth insights.

## Key Modules

### 1. Alchemy Pharmacy
* **The Concept**: The user is the alchemist; the app is the pharmacy. Compound raw life tensions (*prima materia*) through the classical Heptagram (7-7-7) planetary and metallic pivots.
* **Outputs**: Generates grounded operational tinctures designed to settle mental noise, highlight current virtues, and steer clear of common reactionary traps.

### 2. Books of Knowledge Corpus
* **Full-Length Canonical Libraries**: Ingests and searches unabridged core manuscripts including:
  * King James Bible
  * The Book of Enoch (R.H. Charles translation)
  * Nag Hammadi Codices (Gospel of Thomas, Truth, Philip, Secret Book of John)

  * The Kybalion (Three Initiates)
* **Full Scripture & Verse Ingestion**: Verse blocks and complete scriptural passages are preserved intact rather than clipped mid-sentence.
* **Manuscript Upload Support**: Upload your own custom `.txt` or `.md` texts for real-time string analysis and lineage gematria extraction.

### 3. Timeline Forecast
* **9-Year Milestone Cycles**: Tracks personal year cycles based on Pythagorean cycle math.
* **Thematic Guidance**: Details the active life phase—from foundational inception and relationship adjustments to deep study and closure.

### 4. Decan Oracle
* **36 Faces of the Ecliptic**: Explores the three decans for each zodiac sign, complete with traditional planetary sub-rulers, corresponding Minor Arcana Tarot stations, and operational directives.

### 5. Compatibility Matrix
* **Life Path Resonance**: Evaluates the energetic difference between two independent birth dates.
* **Dynamic Tension vs. Accord**: Distinguishes between shared baseline wavelengths, growth-oriented tension, and natural accord.

### 6. Spatiotemporal Frequency & The Crystal Sophia Mirror
* **Map 1: Individual Sacred Geometry**: Renders a dynamic 19-circle Flower of Life matrix and the 10-sphere Tree of Life (*Etz Chaim*), plotting individual frequency nodes based on birth date, diurnal cycle, and geographic coordinates.
* **Map 2: The Crystal Sophia Mirror**: Visualizes the dual-chalice hourglass connecting solar projection with deep lunar waters over the subterranean roots.
* **Everyday Language Reading**: Translates complex polarity mathematics into straightforward relationship dynamics:
  * **Everyday Chemistry**: How you communicate and where shared blind spots lie.
  * **The Practical Bridge**: The exact mindset needed to defuse arguments and reset alignment.
  * **Operating Styles**: How each partner's natural disposition functions without friction.

### 7. Universal Pattern & Multi-Lingual Frequency Engine
* **Multi-Lingual Ciphers**: Automatically identifies script lineages and calculates traditional sum and digital root values for:
  * Latin & Spanish (Pythagorean)
  * Hebrew & Aramaic (Standard Gematria)
  * Greek (Isopsephy)
  * Cyrillic
* **Root Deconstruction**: Interprets core vibrational monad roots (1–9, 11, 22, 33).

### 8. Grand Synthesis Dossier
* **One-Click Export**: Compiles live session data across all chambers—real-time sky stations, Eve's Calendar calculations, alchemy tinctures, timeline sequences, and synastry verdicts.
* **Export Options**: Download as a formatted `.txt` codex or print a styled PDF directly from the browser.
