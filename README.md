# 💥 Numberin: Celestial & Numerical Resonance Workbench

**Numberin 💥** is an interactive Streamlit application uniting historical astronomy, lunar phase cycles, Ptolemaic decan systems, and vibrational numerology into a unified research workbench.

---

## ✨ Features

### 1. 🌌 Milestone Timeline Lens
* **Sacred & Historical Chronology:** Explore pivotal historical, apocalyptic, and cosmic turning points (e.g., the Crucifixion Blood Moon of 33 AD, the Annus Lucis epoch, the 1914 WW1 outbreak, and the Great Year Precessional Alignment).
* **Astronomical Precision:** Calculates historical Julian Day Numbers (JDN), lunar phase states, and illumination percentages across ancient and modern epochs.
* **Personal Resonance Engine:** Cross-references the observer's natal vibration root with the event's numerical signature to assess harmonic alignment, mirror dynamics, and catalytic tension.

### 2. ♈ Decan Oracle & Radial Coordinates
* **Ptolemaic Decan System:** Maps out 30° zodiac arcs subdivided into 10° decans alongside their classical Chaldean and Ptolemaic planetary rulers.
* **Lunar Highlights:** Automatically flags lunar-governed decans (e.g., Taurus Decan 2, Cancer Decan 3) for cycle and shift tracking.

### 3. 🔢 Custom Date & Phase Calculator
* **Ad-Hoc Celestial Lookup:** Select any modern or historical date to determine its exact lunar phase, moon age, illumination percentage, and root vibrational frequency.
